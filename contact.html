What can you contact us about?

You may contact us regarding:

  • General app support
  • Calculation or display feedback
  • Privacy and data-use questions
  • Technical issues or bug reports
  • Feature suggestions
  • Legal or policy-related questions
Please include a short description of your issue and, where relevant, basic device or app information. Do not send passwords, OTPs or other sensitive credentials by email.
